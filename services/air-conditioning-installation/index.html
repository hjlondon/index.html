Frequently asked questions

How long does a commercial installation take?

A single split system is typically installed and commissioned in one day. Multi-room or VRF projects usually run two to five days, and we stage the work around your trading hours — including nights and weekends — so the premises keep operating.

Which brands do you install?

We are a Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Diamond Dealer, so MHI is our standard recommendation and carries an extended manufacturer warranty through our accreditation. Where the building or budget suits a different manufacturer, we will say so in the quote.

Do I need landlord or planning permission for the outdoor unit?

Usually landlord consent rather than planning permission, but listed buildings and conservation areas can differ. We flag any consent requirements at survey stage and provide the technical drawings and noise data your landlord or local authority asks for.

What does a new system cost?

It depends on floor area, heat load and pipework runs, which is why we quote fixed after a free survey rather than guessing over the phone. You get one written price within 24 hours — it doesn't move unless the scope does.

Can you replace an old R22 or R410A system?

Yes. R22 is banned for servicing and R410A is being phased down, so replacement is usually more economic than repair. We recover the old refrigerant with certified equipment, dispose of the plant legally and can often reuse existing pipework after pressure testing.
