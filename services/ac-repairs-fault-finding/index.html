What's included

A breakdown visit is diagnosis first, parts second — you approve a fixed price before repair work starts:

  • 24/7 emergency call-out — a real engineer on the phone, not a message service, with a 4-hour response target
  • Full fault diagnosis — error-code history, electrical testing, pressures and temperatures, not parts-swapping on a hunch
  • Refrigerant leak detection and repair — electronic leak detection, F-Gas certified repair, pressure test and recharge
  • Component replacement — compressors, fan motors, PCBs, sensors and pumps, with common parts carried in the van
  • Certified refrigerant recovery and recharge — weighed, recorded and added to your F-Gas register
  • Post-repair test report — evidence the fault is fixed, plus the cause and how to stop it recurring

Frequently asked questions

How fast can you get an engineer to us?

Our response target is 4 hours across South East London and Kent, 24/7. Maintenance-contract clients are prioritised in the queue. When you call we tell you honestly when an engineer will arrive — no "he's on his way" games.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

There's a fixed call-out and diagnosis charge, quoted when you call, which covers getting an engineer to site and finding the fault. Repairs are then quoted fixed before we start — you always know the full cost before committing.

Repair or replace — how do you decide?

We quote both when it's close. As a rule: a compressor failure on a system over ten years old, or anything running obsolete R22 refrigerant, usually justifies replacement; most other faults are economic repairs. The written diagnosis gives you the evidence either way.

My system runs R22 — can it still be repaired?

R22 has been banned for servicing since 2015, so any repair needing refrigerant work is illegal on that gas. We can still fix electrical and mechanical faults, but if the circuit has to be opened, replacement is the only compliant route — we'll quote it fixed.

Can you attend outside trading hours?

Yes — 24/7 call-out is exactly that, and for planned repairs we routinely work evenings and weekends so restaurants, shops and clinics don't lose trading time.
