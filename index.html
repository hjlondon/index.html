Demand for on-site power is compounding. Data center load growth, industrial electrification, and an aging grid are pushing American energy users toward generation they control — cogeneration, microgrids, and resilient behind-the-meter plants.

Supply of people is not. The technicians and engineers who commission, control, and operate gas engines and turbines are retiring faster than they are being replaced. Every packager, ESCO, and service provider in the country is competing for the same shrinking bench.

That gap is where we work. Prime Mover Search maintains a continuously mapped network of on-site power professionals across the United States — so when you need a commissioning engineer in Texas or a sales director in the Northeast, the search starts warm, not cold.
