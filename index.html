The market brief

Infrastructure spend is climbing. The technician pipeline is shrinking. That's the hiring problem we solve.

Demand for iron is compounding. Infrastructure spending, reshored construction, and an aging installed base are pushing fleets toward replacement and expansion — across construction, mining, and rental equipment alike.

Supply of technicians is not. The diesel and hydraulic technicians who keep this equipment running are retiring faster than trade schools can replace them. Every dealer, rental house, and contractor in the country is competing for the same shrinking bench.

That gap is where we work. Yellow Iron Search maintains a continuously mapped network of heavy equipment professionals across the U.S. and Canada — so when you need a diesel technician in Texas or a branch manager in Ontario, the search starts warm, not cold.
