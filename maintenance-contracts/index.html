F-Gas leak checks: what the law requires

The F-Gas Regulation (retained in UK law as GB F-Gas) sets mandatory leak-check frequencies for any system whose refrigerant charge reaches 5 tonnes of CO2-equivalent — which includes most commercial split and VRF systems. The check must be carried out by certified personnel, and records kept of every check, repair and refrigerant movement.

Refrigerant charge (CO2-equivalent)Minimum leak-check frequencyWith automatic leak detection
5 – 50 tonnesEvery 12 monthsEvery 24 months
50 – 500 tonnesEvery 6 monthsEvery 12 months
500+ tonnesEvery 3 monthsEvery 6 months

As a working example: a typical 10 kW split system holding around 3 kg of R410A carries roughly 6.3 tonnes CO2-equivalent — over the threshold, so an annual certified leak check is a legal requirement, not a nice-to-have. Every Supreme Cooling contract builds the correct frequency for your systems into the visit schedule and maintains the register for you.

Not sure what your systems hold? The free survey includes a refrigerant charge assessment for every unit, so you know exactly where you stand before you spend anything.
