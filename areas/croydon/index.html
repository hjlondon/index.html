Commercial AC services in Croydon

Croydon has more commercial floorspace than anywhere else in South London, and most of it depends on air conditioning that somebody has to keep running. That somebody is often us: we're 25 minutes away via the A232, well inside our 4-hour emergency response target.

Around East Croydon and the business district, the typical job is office floors — VRF and multi-split systems serving open plans, meeting rooms and comms rooms, where a failed condenser in July becomes a staff-welfare issue by lunchtime. We maintain, repair and replace these systems, and we're direct about the repair-or-replace call when kit is past economic life.

Retail and hospitality around Centrale, North End and Boxpark bring different problems: door-open trading that fights the cooling all day, kitchen heat loads, and strict trading-hours windows for any work. We install and service around opening times — early mornings, overnights and Sundays included.

Server and comms rooms are a Croydon speciality of ours — see the case study below for a duty/standby configuration that ended a client's thermal shutdowns for good. If your business runs on a hot little room full of hardware, ask us about redundancy before it fails, not after.
