Commercial AC services in Bromley

Bromley is our home patch. Supreme Cooling Services is based in Chislehurst, BR7 — which means a Bromley call-out is a 15-minute drive, not a trek around the M25, and our 4-hour emergency response target is comfortably met across the borough day or night.

The work here is varied and we've seen most of it: retail units around The Glades and the High Street that need cooling to hold temperature on busy Saturdays without freezing the fitting rooms; office suites from Bromley South to Elmfield Park where meeting rooms overheat long before the open plan does; restaurant and café kitchens along East Street and Widmore Road where extract and cooling have to work together; and dental and GP practices that need documented, compliant systems for inspection evidence.

For landlords and managing agents with Bromley portfolios, we run multi-unit maintenance contracts with per-site reporting — one renewal date, every unit leak-checked at the legally required F-Gas frequency, certificates filed after every visit.
